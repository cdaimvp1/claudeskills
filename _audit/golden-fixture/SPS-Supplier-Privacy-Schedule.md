# SUPPLIER PRIVACY SCHEDULE (SPS)

**SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT.** Incorporated into the Master Services Agreement between Eli
Lilly and Company and Meridian Analytics Systems, Inc. under Section 9.1.

---

## 1. Roles

1.1 Lilly is the Controller. Supplier acts solely as Processor and will process Personal
Information only on Lilly's documented instructions.

## 2. Scope

2.1 This Schedule applies to all Personal Information processed by or on behalf of Lilly.

2.2 "Personal Information" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable
natural person, construed to the broadest extent required by any applicable law, including
the GDPR, the CCPA as amended, and any applicable local law.

2.3 Categories of data subject covered: Lilly employees, contractors, clinical trial
participants, healthcare professionals, and patients participating in a Lilly patient
support programme.

## 3. Purpose Limitation

3.1 Supplier will process Personal Information only to deliver the Services. Supplier will
not process it for its own purposes, for service improvement, for benchmarking, or for any
analytics offered to any other customer.

3.2 Supplier will not use Personal Information to train, fine-tune, or evaluate any model
except as expressly approved in writing under Exhibit C, Section 3.2.

3.3 Aggregation or anonymisation does not remove data from this Schedule unless the result
is irreversibly anonymous under the applicable law's standard.

## 4. Sub-processing

4.1 Supplier will not engage any Sub-processor without Lilly's prior written consent.

4.2 Supplier will give Lilly not less than thirty (30) days' notice before engaging a new
Sub-processor, and Lilly may object.

4.3 Supplier will impose on every Sub-processor data-protection obligations equivalent to
those in this Schedule, and remains liable for each Sub-processor's acts and omissions.

4.4 Any AI Provider is a Sub-processor for the purposes of this Schedule.

## 5. Security

5.1 Supplier will encrypt Personal Information in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher, and at
rest using AES-256 or an equivalent approved algorithm.

5.2 Supplier will apply role-based access control, least privilege, and multi-factor
authentication for all access to Lilly Information.

5.3 Supplier will maintain an incident response plan and will test it at least annually.

## 6. Security Incidents

6.1 Supplier will notify Lilly of a Security Incident without undue delay and in any event
within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware.

6.2 The notification will state the nature of the incident, the categories and approximate
number of data subjects affected, the likely consequences, and the measures taken.

## 7. Data Subject Rights

7.1 Supplier will assist Lilly in responding to any data subject request within ten (10)
business days, at no additional charge.

7.2 Supplier will be able to delete an individual record on request, not only in bulk.

## 8. International Transfer

8.1 Supplier will not transfer Personal Information outside the United States without
Lilly's prior written consent and an approved transfer mechanism.

8.2 Where the data subject is located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom,
the Standard Contractual Clauses apply regardless of where processing physically occurs.

## 9. Audit

9.1 Lilly may audit Supplier's processing on thirty (30) days' notice, and at any time for
cause.

9.2 Supplier will provide its current SOC 2 Type II report annually. The report supplements
and does not replace Lilly's audit right.

## 10. Return and Deletion

10.1 On termination Supplier will return or securely destroy all Personal Information
within ninety (90) days, in a usable format, and will certify destruction in writing.

10.2 Supplier will retain no copies, including anonymised or aggregated copies, except
where retention is required by law.

10.3 Supplier will delete Personal Information from backups within a reasonable period
consistent with its documented backup cycle.
