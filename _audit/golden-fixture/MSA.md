# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT. Not a real contract. Not legal advice. Do not use as a template.**

Between Eli Lilly and Company ("Lilly") and Meridian Analytics Systems, Inc. ("Supplier").
Effective Date: 1 March 2024. Lilly paper, MPT 5.0 template.

---

## 1. Term and Renewal

1.1 The initial term of this Agreement is three (3) years from the Effective Date.

1.2 Lilly may renew this Agreement for successive one (1) year terms at its sole
discretion by written notice given not less than thirty (30) days before expiry.
Supplier has no right to renew, and no renewal occurs automatically.

1.3 Rates during any renewal term are governed by Exhibit B, Section 4.1.

## 2. Scope of Services

2.1 Supplier will provide the services described in each Work Order executed under this
Agreement. No Work Order expands the scope of this Agreement, and Supplier has no right
to add deliverables or expand scope unilaterally.

## 3. Fees and Payment

3.1 Lilly will pay undisputed invoices within forty-five (45) days of receipt of a
correct invoice (Net-45).

3.2 Rates are set out in the rate card at Exhibit B, Section 3. Annual rate escalation,
if any, is capped at three percent (3%) and must be tied to CPI-U or an equivalent
published index.

3.3 No advance payment or payment on receipt is permitted.

## 4. Confidentiality

4.1 Each party will protect the other's Confidential Information using no less than
reasonable care, and will not disclose it except to personnel with a need to know.

4.2 Confidentiality obligations survive termination of this Agreement for five (5) years.

4.3 "Confidential Information" is defined at Exhibit A, Section 1.

## 5. Intellectual Property

5.1 All Work Product created by Supplier in performance of the Services is assigned to
Lilly on creation. "Work Product" is defined at Exhibit A, Section 2.

5.2 Supplier retains its pre-existing intellectual property and grants Lilly a
non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free licence to use it to the extent embedded in the
Work Product.

5.3 Ownership of trained models and model outputs is governed by Exhibit C, Section 15.

## 7. Representations and Warranties

7.1 Each party represents that it has authority to enter this Agreement.

7.2 Supplier represents that the Services will be performed in a professional and
workmanlike manner consistent with prevailing industry standards.

7.3 Supplier represents that it will comply with all applicable laws in performing the
Services.

## 8. Taxes

8.1 Each party is responsible for its own taxes. Lilly may withhold amounts required by
applicable law.

8.2 Lilly retains the right to disclose this Agreement and any Work Order to any tax
authority having jurisdiction, without restriction and without notice to Supplier.

## 9. Data Protection

9.1 Where Supplier processes Personal Information on Lilly's behalf, the Supplier Privacy
Schedule (SPS) applies and is incorporated by reference.

9.2 Supplier will notify Lilly of any Security Incident affecting Lilly Information
without undue delay and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware.

## 11. Audit Rights

11.1 Lilly may audit Supplier's compliance with this Agreement on thirty (30) days'
notice, not more than once per calendar year absent cause.

11.2 Third-party attestations (SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001) supplement but do not replace
Lilly's direct audit right.

## 12. Compliance with Laws

12.1 Supplier will comply with all applicable laws, including the U.S. Foreign Corrupt
Practices Act and the UK Bribery Act 2010.

## 14. Insurance

14.1 Supplier will maintain, at minimum:

| Coverage | Minimum limit |
|---|---|
| Commercial General Liability | $2,000,000 per occurrence |
| Professional Liability / Errors and Omissions | $5,000,000 |
| Workers Compensation | Statutory |
| Automobile Liability | $1,000,000 |
| Cyber Liability (where Supplier accesses Lilly data) | $5,000,000 |

## 15. Force Majeure

15.1 Neither party is liable for delay caused by an event beyond its reasonable control.
Force Majeure does not excuse any payment obligation, and does not include labour
disputes affecting only the claiming party or that party's financial difficulty.

15.2 Lilly may terminate any affected Work Order if a Force Majeure event continues for
more than ninety (90) days.

## 16. Termination

16.1 Lilly may terminate this Agreement or any Work Order for convenience on thirty (30)
days' written notice.

16.2 Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within thirty (30) days of
written notice.

## 17. Indemnification

17.1 Supplier will indemnify Lilly against third-party claims arising from Supplier's
negligence, infringement of intellectual property, breach of confidentiality, breach of
data protection obligations, or violation of applicable law.

17.2 Lilly will indemnify Supplier against third-party claims arising from Lilly's
negligence.

## 18. Limitation of Liability

18.1 Each party's aggregate liability is capped at the greater of five million dollars
($5,000,000) or the fees paid under the applicable Work Order in the twelve (12) months
preceding the claim.

18.2 The cap does not apply to breach of confidentiality, indemnification obligations,
data breach, or wilful misconduct.

## 19. Artificial Intelligence

19.1 Where Supplier uses an Automated System in performing the Services, the Lilly
Artificial Intelligence Standard at Exhibit C applies and is incorporated by reference.

## 20. Assignment

20.1 Lilly may assign this Agreement to any affiliate without Supplier's consent.
Supplier may not assign without Lilly's prior written consent. A change of control of
Supplier is deemed an assignment.

## 23. Adverse Event Reporting

23.1 Supplier will report any adverse event, product complaint, or safety information
concerning a Lilly product of which it becomes aware to the Lilly Answers Center within
one (1) business day of becoming aware.

23.2 Supplier will train relevant personnel on this obligation and will maintain that
training for the duration of this Agreement.

23.3 This Section 23 survives termination of this Agreement.

## 24. Debarment

24.1 Supplier certifies that neither it nor any of its personnel performing Services is
debarred, excluded, or otherwise ineligible under 21 U.S.C. 335a or any comparable
authority.

24.2 Supplier will notify Lilly immediately upon any change in that status, and Lilly may
terminate immediately on such notice.

## 25. Trade Sanctions and Export Control

25.1 Supplier represents that it is not, and is not owned or controlled by, a party
identified on any restricted-party list maintained by OFAC, the U.S. Department of
Commerce, the European Union, or the United Kingdom.

25.2 Supplier will screen its personnel and subcontractors against those lists and will
notify Lilly immediately if any party becomes listed.

25.3 Supplier will comply with the U.S. Export Administration Regulations and the
International Traffic in Arms Regulations to the extent applicable.

## 26. Governing Law

26.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Indiana, without regard to
its conflict of laws provisions.

## 27. Dispute Resolution

27.1 The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts
located in Marion County, Indiana.
