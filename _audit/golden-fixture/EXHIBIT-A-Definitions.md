# EXHIBIT A — DEFINITIONS

**SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT.** Incorporated into the Master Services Agreement between Eli
Lilly and Company and Meridian Analytics Systems, Inc.

---

## 1. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means non-public information disclosed by one party to the
other, in any form, that is designated confidential or that a reasonable person would
understand to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure. It
includes Lilly Information.

Excluded: information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving party;
was rightfully known to the receiving party before disclosure; or is independently
developed without reference to the disclosing party's Confidential Information.

## 2. Work Product

"Work Product" means all deliverables, reports, analyses, software, documentation, data
compilations, and other materials created by Supplier in the performance of the Services,
whether or not identified in a Work Order, together with all intellectual property rights
in them.

## 3. Lilly Information

"Lilly Information" means all data, records, and information provided by or on behalf of
Lilly to Supplier, or generated by Supplier in the performance of the Services, including
derivatives, aggregations, and analytical outputs derived from it.

## 4. Usage Data

"Usage Data" means telemetry generated by Supplier's platform describing system
performance, feature utilisation, and error rates. Usage Data excludes Lilly Information
and excludes any human-authored content submitted to the platform by Lilly personnel.

## 5. Lilly Property

"Lilly Property" means Lilly Information, Work Product, Lilly's trademarks, and any
materials Lilly provides to Supplier for use in performing the Services.

## 6. Services Supportive Technology

"Services Supportive Technology" means any software, platform, tool, model, or automated
capability used by Supplier to perform or support the Services, including any Automated
System as defined in Exhibit C.

## 7. Subcontractor

"Subcontractor" means any third party engaged by Supplier to perform any part of the
Services, including any provider of Services Supportive Technology that processes Lilly
Information.
