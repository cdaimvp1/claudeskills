# EXHIBIT C — LILLY ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE STANDARD (extract)

**SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT.** Incorporated into the Master Services Agreement between Eli
Lilly and Company and Meridian Analytics Systems, Inc. This extract carries only the
provisions the Work Order touches.

---

## 3.1 Definitions

"Automated System" means any system that uses machine learning, statistical modelling, or
comparable technique to generate an output, recommendation, classification, or decision
with limited or no human intervention at the point of output.

"AI Provider" means any third party that supplies an Automated System, a model, or model
hosting used in performing the Services.

"High-Impact Use" means use of an Automated System where the output materially informs a
decision affecting patient safety, product quality, regulatory submission, or an
individual's rights.

"Lilly Automated Property" means any model, weights, embeddings, fine-tuning artefacts, or
derived model capability created using Lilly Content or Lilly Information.

## 3.2 Permitted Uses

Supplier may use an Automated System only as expressly described in an approved Work
Order. Any use not described requires Lilly's prior written approval.

## 3.4 Lilly Content

Supplier may use Lilly Content solely to deliver the Services to Lilly. Supplier may not
use Lilly Content to train, fine-tune, or improve any model that is available to, or whose
outputs are available to, any party other than Lilly.

## 3.6 Third-Party AI Providers as Subcontractors

Every AI Provider is a Subcontractor for all purposes of the Agreement, including the
data-protection flow-down obligations of the SPS, unless the use qualifies as a Low-Impact
Use under Section 3.3. Supplier must disclose each AI Provider before it processes any
Lilly Information and must obtain Lilly's approval.

## 3.10 Storage and Purging

On cessation of an Automated System's use for Lilly, Supplier will delete all Lilly
Content and all Lilly Automated Property from that system and will certify the deletion in
writing within thirty (30) days.

## 15. Ownership of Outputs and Trained Models

15.1 All outputs generated by an Automated System in performing the Services are Work
Product and are assigned to Lilly under Section 5.1 of the Agreement.

15.2 All Lilly Automated Property is owned by Lilly. Supplier acquires no right in it and
may not retain, reuse, or license it.
