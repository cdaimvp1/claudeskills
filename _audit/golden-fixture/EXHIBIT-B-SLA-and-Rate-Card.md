# EXHIBIT B — SERVICE LEVELS AND RATE CARD

**SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT.** Incorporated into the Master Services Agreement between Eli
Lilly and Company and Meridian Analytics Systems, Inc.

---

## 1. Availability Service Level

1.1 Supplier will maintain platform availability of not less than **99.9%** measured
monthly, excluding scheduled maintenance notified at least five (5) business days in
advance.

1.2 Availability below 99.9% in any month entitles Lilly to a service credit of 5% of
that month's platform fee per 0.1% shortfall, to a maximum of 30%.

## 2. Support Response

2.1 Severity 1 (production down): acknowledged within one (1) hour, twenty-four hours a
day, seven days a week.

2.2 Severity 2 (major function impaired): acknowledged within four (4) business hours.

2.3 Severity 3 (all other): acknowledged within one (1) business day.

## 3. Rate Card

Rates are fully loaded and inclusive of all overhead. No rate may be exceeded in any Work
Order without a written amendment to this Exhibit.

| Role | Rate | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| Principal Data Architect | $285.00 | per hour |
| Senior Data Engineer | $210.00 | per hour |
| Data Engineer | $165.00 | per hour |
| Analytics Consultant | $175.00 | per hour |
| Project Manager | $145.00 | per hour |
| Technical Writer | $95.00 | per hour |

3.1 Pass-through expenses (travel, subcontractor cost, equipment) are billed at cost plus
a markup not exceeding ten percent (10%).

3.2 All roles are quoted per hour. A Work Order quoting a different unit of measure for
the same role requires a written amendment to this Exhibit.

## 4. Rate Protection

4.1 Rates in Section 3 are locked for the initial three (3) year term. Any increase at
renewal is capped at three percent (3%) annually and must be tied to CPI-U or an
equivalent published index. Rates may not increase during the initial term.

## 5. Acceptance Criteria

5.1 Every deliverable in a Work Order must state its acceptance criteria, its definition
of completion, and its delivery date. A deliverable stated without all three is not
accepted and is not payable.
