## 1. Scope of Services

1.1 Supplier will support implementation of the clinical data platform, assist with data
migration activities as needed, and provide advisory services in relation to analytics
enablement.

1.2 Supplier will deliver the following:

| # | Deliverable | Delivery |
|---|---|---|
| D-1 | Migration approach documentation | On request |
| D-2 | Data platform configuration | As needed during the term |
| D-3 | Analytics enablement support | Ongoing |
| D-4 | Migration execution per Exhibit E (Data Migration Plan) | Per Exhibit E |

1.3 Supplier will process clinical trial participant data and patient support programme
data in performing the Services.

1.4 Supplier will use its Meridian Insight automated analytics capability, which
incorporates a third-party large language model provider, to generate cohort summaries and
anomaly flags for clinical operations review.
