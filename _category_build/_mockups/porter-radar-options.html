Supply-market forces

You could not tell what the five points meant, and you were right: only the top one was labelled and a caption asked you to count clockwise in your head. Three ways to fix it, including your overlay idea, which I think is the best of them.

The five points, for reference
Rivalryhow hard existing suppliers compete with each other Supplier powerhow much leverage the seller holds over you Substituteswhether the need could be met a different way New entrantswhether new competitors are arriving Buyer powerhow much leverage you hold

Further toward a point means that force is stronger. Strong rivalry and strong buyer power are good for you; strong supplier power is not.

Option A — one overlaid radar, reads as cardsRecommended your idea

All segments on one labelled frame, so the comparison is the chart rather than something you do by looking back and forth. The shapes are the finding: where one segment bulges toward supplier power and another toward buyer power, the two markets are opposites and the plays cannot be the same. Each read becomes a card beside it, colour-matched to its outline.

Keeps the small multiples and fixes only the labelling: one labelled outline acts as the key, and the three shapes read against it. Less change, but the comparison is still done by eye across three frames rather than in one.

Option C — no radar at all, scored bars most readable, least distinctive

A radar's real weakness is that reading an exact value off it is hard. Bars per force, grouped by segment, are unambiguous and need no key. You lose the shape, which is what makes two markets look obviously different at a glance. I would only take this if the panel has to be read on a phone or printed in mono.

What I would build

Option A. It answers the question you actually asked, because the axes are labelled on the frame instead of in a caption, and it makes the comparison structural rather than manual. It also takes less vertical space than three separate charts, which matters on a panel that already sits beside Kraljic. Selecting a segment on the toggle would fade the others rather than replacing the chart.
