RFx Recommendation Tab, Panel Options

Two to three layout options for each of five panels on the RFx Recommendation subtab, so a preferred direction can be picked before anything gets built. This is a reflect-only mockup: nothing here scores, sends, advances, or awards anything. Every number is drawn from the same illustrative RFx used elsewhere in the product, the Enterprise Data Platform RFP, evaluating Nimbus Data, Lakehouse Co, and Helio Warehouse.
Reading the data: Nimbus Data is the top panel score (4.4 of 5) and the lowest, most transparent price ($980,000/yr), but it holds a SOC 2 Type I report against a Must-Have Type II requirement, an open gate. Lakehouse Co scores second on the panel (4.2 of 5) at $1,060,000/yr but clears every Must-Have, the clean conforming alternative. Helio Warehouse (3.2 of 5) has the field's strongest SAP integration but submitted no price, no MSA, and no implementation plan, an incomplete bid. These facts recur across every option below so the choice between layouts, not the underlying data, is what's being evaluated.

The Case, Per Supplier

The argument for and against each bidder, in the order the advisory ranking places them, so the panel can see not just the score but the reasoning behind it. Each option below carries the same underlying case, written as real narrative prose rather than bare bullets, just organized differently.
A
Accordion, narrative plus for and against
One open at a time; best for a focused read of the leading case without three arguments competing for attention.
1 Nimbus Data Leader, gate risk For: Top panel score and lowest price in the field   Against: Open SOC 2 Type II gate
Nimbus Data is Theo's advisory recommendation on panel score, the strongest functional and commercial submission in the field. It posts full marks on data modeling, ETL, and self-service analytics, and its price, $980,000 a year, is both the lowest in the field and the most transparent, a 31% cut from list held firm for 90 days. Weighted fit lands at 80 of 100 with 79% of requirements fully met. Set against that is one hard stop: Nimbus holds a SOC 2 Type I report, not the Type II the RFP names as a Must-Have, so the award stays barred until a current Type II is in hand or the panel accepts the risk. Behind the gate sit three lesser, non-fatal concerns: an unproven SAP integration path through middleware, a venture-stage balance sheet without audited financials, and no pharmaceutical reference in the set.

2 Lakehouse Co Clean alternative For: Clears every Must-Have, highest weighted fit   Against: $80,000/yr above Nimbus
Lakehouse Co is the clean alternative, the highest-scored bidder that clears every Must-Have. It is field-best on security and compliance, full SOC 2 Type II and FedRAMP Moderate, and it carries the strongest regulated-industry reference base plus an existing Lilly relationship, one active MSA on file and $2.4M in trailing spend. Weighted fit is the highest in the field at 88 of 100 with 86% of requirements fully met. The trade-off is commercial: its price, $1,060,000 a year, is $80,000 above Nimbus, and total cost of ownership is its comparatively weakest category. If the panel wants zero gate risk rather than a business call, Lakehouse is the supplier that delivers it today.

3 Helio Warehouse Incomplete bid For: Strongest SAP integration   Against: No price, MSA, or plan submitted
Helio Warehouse brings the field's strongest SAP and S/4HANA integration, native adapters rather than middleware, against the exact backbone Lilly runs, plus the lowest projected run cost through consumption-based pricing. That strength sits inside an incomplete bid: Helio submitted no firm price, no MSA redline, and no implementation plan, and left the AI-roadmap requirement unanswered, leaving coverage at 43%, the lowest in the field. It cannot be scored on cost or ranked against Nimbus and Lakehouse until the missing sections arrive. The right posture today is to request the outstanding items and hold Helio as a strategic third option, not to award to it on the current submission.
