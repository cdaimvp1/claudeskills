Design exploration, round 4, one panel only, not a change to the live dashboard

RFx Recommendation Tab, Model the Decision, Layout Variants

Three refined layouts for a single panel: Model the Decision, the award-scenario modeler on the Recommendation tab. The left column keeps the compact category-weight sliders from the round-2 merged option, already approved. The right side is now always a vertical stack, never a side-by-side middle column, and the sensitivity radar is back to the three-scenario version, "Base weights (filed)," "Cost-priority," and "Security-priority," overlaid with their short descriptions, not the two-polygon filed-versus-live version. The ranking block shows supplier scores only, no bar graphs, with a one-line leader read and a one-line "closest lever" sensitivity read, and it now sits above the radar in most variants. The legend has been minimized and moved underneath the chart. Reflect-only: the panel's real weights and scores never change, this is a what-if sandbox layered on top of them.
Reading the data: Nimbus Data is the top panel score (4.4 of 5) and the lowest, most transparent price ($980,000/yr), but it holds a SOC 2 Type I report against a Must-Have Type II requirement, an open gate, so it is excluded from the ranking regardless of weights. Lakehouse Co scores second on the panel (4.2 of 5) at $1,060,000/yr and clears every Must-Have. Helio Warehouse (3.2 of 5) has the field's strongest SAP integration but submitted no price, no MSA, and no implementation plan. The category weights and per-supplier fit scores below reuse the same requirement model as the prior two rounds, so all three documents stay consistent.

Single panel, 3 layout variants

Model the Decision

Same sliders, same three-scenario radar, same scores-only ranking, three different orderings of the right-hand stack. In Variants 1 and 3 the ranking reads first, so the score and the "why" land before the shape. In Variant 2 the radar reads first, so the sensitivity picture lands before the numbers. Variant 3 adds a fourth, faint polygon to the radar that tracks the sliders live, so dragging a weight still visibly moves the chart even though the three reference scenarios themselves never move. In every variant, the left-hand sliders drive the ranking's scores live as they are dragged.
