Decision Rationalewhy Nimbus Data, grounded in the response analysis
On the first-pass response analysis, Nimbus Data is the advisory recommendation, the top panel score in the field: weighted fit 80/100, with 79% of requirements fully met. It is strongest where it counts on functional fit, commercial terms and self-service usability, and its recommended commercial baseline is $980,000/yr, the most aggressive price in the field. Lakehouse Co is the next-ranked bidder (4.2/5), clean on every Must-Have. Business call: the panel can proceed with Nimbus and accept the SOC 2 Type II gate risk, or secure a dated remediation before award; Theo does not substitute a lower-scoring bidder for a gate alone. No other open Must-Have gate stands across the conforming field.

The Ask
Award the enterprise data platform contract to Nimbus Data, subject to the SOC 2 Type II condition below and to final contract execution.
Grounded Rationale
why Nimbus, on the panel's own criteria
Nimbus Data returned the highest weighted panel score in the field, 4.4 out of 5, and fully meets 79% of the fourteen RFP requirements, leading the field on functional fit, commercial offer and self-service usability. It does not lead on security & compliance, which is why the SOC 2 gate below stands as a named condition rather than a silent risk. The evaluation policy keeps the top scorer as the advisory recommendation with the gate flagged, not demoted.

Risk & Mitigation
The open item is SOC 2 Type II certification, a Must-Have (weight 5, mandatory) under Security & compliance. Nimbus submitted a Type I report only; per the panel's Q1 clarification to bidders, "a Type I plus a dated Type II roadmap may be clarified but does not satisfy the gate at award." It is the only Must-Have Nimbus fails.
Preferred Mitigation
Secure a dated SOC 2 Type II remediation commitment from Nimbus; hold contract execution until it is received.
Accept-Risk Fallback
Panel documents formal acceptance of the gap and proceeds without remediation; requires explicit sign-off, not a default.
Walk-Away Comparator
Lakehouse Co is clean on every Must-Have today (current SOC 2 Type II + FedRAMP Moderate) at an indicative $1,060,000/yr, about $80,000/yr above Nimbus.
Conditions & Contingencies
  • Nimbus must deliver a dated SOC 2 Type II certification commitment before contract execution proceeds to signature.
  • Negotiation targets the $210,000 fixed-fee implementation quote and confirms the 3%/yr escalator cap survives redline.
  • If Nimbus cannot commit to a dated Type II path, the recommendation reverts to Lakehouse Co, the conforming alternative, per the response analysis.
Approval Sought · Specific To This Session
Panel confirmation of Nimbus Data as the recommended award, and authorization to proceed into contract negotiation. The award itself remains conditional on contract execution and on satisfying the SOC 2 condition above; this is not a final award decision.
