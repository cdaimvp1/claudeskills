Design exploration, round 3, not a change to the live dashboard

RFx Recommendation Tab, Panel Options Round 3

A refinement pass that combines the best-received pieces of the first two rounds into two merged panel designs. Evaluation Readiness pairs the completeness ring from round one with the condensed two-column checklist from round two, shown in two compact arrangements so a layout can be picked. Model the Decision keeps the compact weight sliders and live ranking from round two's merged option, but swaps the sensitivity list for the axis-per-supplier sensitivity radar, live-driven by the same sliders, laid out so the ranking block is never clipped. The other three Recommendation-tab panels are unchanged and not repeated here. Reflect-only: nothing on this page scores, sends, advances, or awards anything.
Reading the data: Nimbus Data is the top panel score (4.4 of 5) and the lowest, most transparent price ($980,000/yr), but it holds a SOC 2 Type I report against a Must-Have Type II requirement, an open gate. Lakehouse Co scores second on the panel (4.2 of 5) at $1,060,000/yr but clears every Must-Have, the clean conforming alternative. Helio Warehouse (3.2 of 5) has the field's strongest SAP integration but submitted no price, no MSA, and no implementation plan, an incomplete bid. The category weights and per-supplier fit scores below reuse the same requirement model as both prior rounds, so all three documents stay consistent.

Model the Decision, Sliders + Radar + Ranking

An interactive award-scenario modeler. Adjust the category weights on the left and two things move together: the sensitivity radar, one axis per supplier, and the ranking under these weights on the right, laid out in three columns so the ranking is never clipped, at any reasonable width.
