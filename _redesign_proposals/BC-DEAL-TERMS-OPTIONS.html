Deal Terms & The Field

The current render stacks a plain Current Deal Terms key/value list directly above a full-width Supplier Comparison table, both full width. Minimum fix: Supplier Comparison moves beside Current Deal Terms, side by side. All three options below meet that minimum and go further, each with a distinct approach to making both halves earn their space.
Reading the data: Nimbus Data is the recommended supplier: panel score 4.4 of 5, $980,000/yr (31% below the $1.42M list, held firm 90 days), but it carries the RFP's only Must-Have gate risk (SOC 2 Type II) and is flagged Critical risk on that basis. Lakehouse Co scores 4.2 of 5 at $1,060,000/yr, Medium risk, and is gate-clear. Helio Warehouse scores 3.2 of 5, High risk, gate-clear, and submitted no price, no term, and no escalator, only a pricing model is on file. All figures are as submitted, pre-negotiation, reflect-only.

All figures for Nimbus Data, Lakehouse Co and Helio Warehouse are as submitted in the pricing schedule (seed project-view.js) and reflected by the live rfxBcDealTermsHTML / rfxBcSupplierComparisonHTML renderers in pv-09-rfx.js; TCO and delta figures in Options B and C are indicative, pre-award reads layered on those figures for illustration, not new submitted facts. Missing Helio figures are marked NEEDS_INPUT rather than estimated.
