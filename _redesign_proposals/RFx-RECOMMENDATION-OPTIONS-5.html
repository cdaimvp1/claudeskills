Sensitivity radar, three scenarios
Design-exploration mockup, round 5, compact pass on one panel ("Model the Decision" on the RFx Recommendation tab), built from the Variant 1 structure in RFx-RECOMMENDATION-OPTIONS-4.html (sliders left, ranking then radar then mini legend on the right). Same data, weights, and scoring logic; the radar's SVG viewBox is now computed from the shape's own rendered bounding box on every draw, so it hugs the axes and spoke labels instead of sitting in a fixed oversized canvas. Not connected to live data and not part of the shipped dashboard. Colors follow the MCM palette: plum as primary, teal as secondary, burnt orange as the single emphasis accent, deep rust reserved for blocked or critical states, muted blue for neutral information.
