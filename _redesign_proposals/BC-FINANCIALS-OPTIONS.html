Business Case: Financials, 3 Compact Options

The current Financials section (full-width 3-yr spend pro-forma table, a large cumulative cash-flow line chart, and a savings waterfall) is oversized for what it shows and too thin, it prices only the recommended supplier, Nimbus Data. These 3 options replace it with a medium-detail financial case built from the kind of data a real RFP response carries: a cost-component build, a multi-supplier TCO comparison, unit economics, an escalator sensitivity, and a compact savings waterfall, laid out three different ways in a fraction of the space.
Grounding. Figures below are the suppliers' own submitted pricing schedules. Nimbus Data: $980,000/yr, per-seat SaaS subscription, 31% off a $1,420,000 list, $210,000 fixed-fee implementation, capped 3%/yr escalator, held firm 90 days, 14 of 14 requirements answered, panel score 4.4/5, SOC 2 Type II gate open. Lakehouse Co: $1,060,000/yr, platform subscription, 10.2% off a $1,180,000 list, $340,000 T&M-estimated implementation, CPI escalator capped 4%/yr, held firm 120 days, 14 of 14 answered, panel score 4.2/5, clears every Must-Have. Helio Warehouse submitted no price, no MSA and no implementation plan; every commercial field reads Not Submitted, 13 of 14 requirements answered, coverage 43%, panel score 3.2/5. Any figure beyond these submitted fields (per-year escalation, cost-component splits, sensitivity, cash timing) is computed for this mockup and labeled Indicative; nothing is fabricated for Helio and no seat count is invented where the schedule does not carry one (Needs Input).

Design-exploration mockup, 3 layout options for the Financials section of the RFx Business Case subtab, replacing the oversized full-width 3-yr pro-forma table, large cumulative cash-flow line chart and savings waterfall in pv-09-rfx.js (rfxBcProformaHTML / rfxBcCashflowHTML / rfxBcWaterfallHTML). All figures are grounded in the suppliers' submitted pricing schedules in the project-view seed; per-year escalation, the illustrative support/training split, the escalator sensitivity and the cash-timing reading are computed for this mockup and marked Indicative, pre-award. Fields the submissions do not carry, Helio Warehouse's price and MSA, and either supplier's seat count, are marked Needs Input or Not Submitted rather than estimated. Not connected to live data and not part of the shipped dashboard. Colors follow the MCM palette only: plum as primary, teal and muted blue as secondary, burnt orange as the single emphasis accent for savings/deltas, deep rust reserved for gap/not-submitted states, grey for neutral needs-input states.
