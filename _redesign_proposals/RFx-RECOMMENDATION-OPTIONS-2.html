Design exploration, round 2, not a change to the live dashboard

RFx Recommendation Tab, Panel Options Round 2

A follow-up pass on two of the five Recommendation-tab panels. Evaluation Readiness keeps every item, status, and piece of evidence from the two directions already liked, condensed into a far smaller footprint. Model the Decision adds a merged sliders-plus-sensitivity layout and two new radar variants that put suppliers, not requirement categories, on the axes. The other three panels (What Happens Next, The Case Per Supplier, Theo-Modeled Alternatives) are unchanged and not repeated here. Reflect-only: nothing on this page scores, sends, advances, or awards anything.
Reading the data: Nimbus Data is the top panel score (4.4 of 5) and the lowest, most transparent price ($980,000/yr), but it holds a SOC 2 Type I report against a Must-Have Type II requirement, an open gate. Lakehouse Co scores second on the panel (4.2 of 5) at $1,060,000/yr but clears every Must-Have, the clean conforming alternative. Helio Warehouse (3.2 of 5) has the field's strongest SAP integration but submitted no price, no MSA, and no implementation plan, an incomplete bid. The category weights and per-supplier fit scores below reuse the same requirement model as the original Model the Decision panel, so both documents stay consistent.

Evaluation Readiness, Condensed

A checklist of the preconditions that should be true before this recommendation is treated as final: bid leveling, complete pricing, resolved gating clarifications, an acknowledged gate conflict, and cleared mandatory reviews. The gated-checklist and completeness-ring directions from the first pass carried the right detail but ran long down the page. All three variants below keep every item, its status, and its evidence; they just cost far less vertical space. In this RFx, only 1 of 5 items clear today.

Panel 2 of 2

Model the Decision, Merged + Radar

Two new directions for the award-scenario modeler. Option A merges the category-weight sliders and the sensitivity read into one panel instead of two. Options B and C flip the usual radar chart around: instead of one axis per requirement category, each axis is a supplier, so the shape traces how a weighting scenario reshuffles the field. Reflect-only: the panel's real weights and scores never change, this is a what-if sandbox layered on top of them.
A
Compact sliders plus live sensitivity read
Narrow weight sliders on the left; a live ranking and a live "which lever flips the leader" read on the right, in one panel. The sliders and the sensitivity list are both live.
