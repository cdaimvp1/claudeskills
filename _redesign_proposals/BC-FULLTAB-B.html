Decision Rationalewhy Nimbus Data, grounded in the response analysis
Nimbus Data is the advisory tier leader with an open gate risk: weighted fit 80 / 100 and 79% of requirements fully met, the strongest functional and commercial submission in the field, but non-conforming on one Must-Have. It leads on data modeling, ETL and self-service analytics (5 of 5 across the board) and carries the lowest price on the table, 31% below list and held firm for 90 days. No gating clarification items remain open across the field. The one unresolved item is the SOC 2 Type II gate: Nimbus submitted a Type I report only, and the RFP requires a current Type II at award. Business call: this is the top-scoring bidder in the field; the panel can proceed with Nimbus and accept the gate risk, or secure a dated remediation before award, or select Lakehouse Co, the highest-scored bidder that clears every Must-Have, as the clean conforming alternative.
Theo’s take: Nimbus stays the recommendation on the panel score, gate flagged, business call for the panel. Lakehouse Co, the gate-pass leader and clean on every Must-Have, is the conforming alternative if the panel prefers to avoid the gate risk rather than accept it or wait on remediation.

Award the enterprise data platform contract to Nimbus Data, subject to the SOC 2 Type II condition below and to final contract execution.

Nimbus Data returned the highest weighted panel score in the field, 4.4 out of 5, and fully meets 79% of the fourteen RFP requirements, leading the field on functional fit, commercial offer and self-service usability. It does not lead on security & compliance, which is why the SOC 2 gate stands as a named condition rather than a silent risk. The evaluation policy keeps the top scorer as the advisory recommendation with the gate flagged, not demoted.

Risk & Mitigation
The open item is SOC 2 Type II certification, a Must-Have (weight 5, mandatory) under Security & compliance. Nimbus submitted a Type I report only; per the panel’s Q1 clarification to bidders, “a Type I plus a dated Type II roadmap may be clarified but does not satisfy the gate at award.” It is the only Must-Have Nimbus fails.
Preferred Mitigation
Secure a dated SOC 2 Type II remediation commitment from Nimbus; hold contract execution until it is received.
Accept-Risk Fallback
Panel documents formal acceptance of the gap and proceeds without remediation; requires explicit sign-off, not a default.
Walk-Away Comparator
Lakehouse Co is clean on every Must-Have today (current SOC 2 Type II + FedRAMP Moderate) at an indicative $1,060,000/yr, about $80,000/yr above Nimbus.
Conditions & Contingencies
  • Nimbus must deliver a dated SOC 2 Type II certification commitment before contract execution proceeds to signature.
  • Negotiation targets the $210,000 fixed-fee implementation quote and confirms the 3%/yr escalator cap survives redline.
  • If Nimbus cannot commit to a dated Type II path, the recommendation reverts to Lakehouse Co, the conforming alternative, per the response analysis.
