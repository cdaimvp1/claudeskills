Context

This RFP ran a competitive process to select an enterprise data platform to consolidate Lilly’s analytics workloads onto one governed platform (~$3.2M three-year TCO planning estimate, Yellow CCI; award is subject to contract). Three suppliers were fully evaluated against fourteen weighted requirements across eight categories: functional fit, integration & architecture, security & compliance, performance & scalability, operational support, total cost, innovation & roadmap, and risk & stability. A fourth supplier, Helio, submitted an incomplete bid, no commercial proposal was included (price NEEDS_INPUT), and is not eligible for award consideration until pricing is on file.

Recommendation

The category team recommends award to Nimbus Data. It returned the field’s highest panel score, 4.4 out of 5, and fully meets 79% of requirements, leading on functional fit, commercial terms and self-service usability. Nimbus carries one open Must-Have gap, SOC 2 Type II certification (submitted at Type I only), carried forward here as a named condition rather than a disqualification; the top-scoring bidder stays the advisory recommendation with the gate flagged, not demoted.

Financial Summary
$980,000/yr indicative · $2,940,000 indicative 3-yr TCO · 31% below list ($1,420,000) · $210,000 fixed-fee implementation · escalator capped 3%/yr · quote held firm 90 days. All figures are Nimbus’s submitted, pre-negotiation proposal.
Risk Posture

Primary risk: the SOC 2 Type II gate. Per the panel’s clarification to bidders (Q1), a Type I report plus a roadmap does not satisfy a Must-Have at award; Nimbus must deliver a current Type II report or a dated remediation commitment. Secondary, non-gating items flagged in the response analysis: SAP/S4HANA integration runs through unproven middleware rather than a native adapter, and Nimbus is a venture-stage vendor on an unaudited balance sheet. The conforming comparator, Lakehouse Co, is clean on every Must-Have (current SOC 2 Type II, FedRAMP Moderate) at an indicative $1,060,000/yr, roughly $80,000/yr above Nimbus, with a shallower 10.2% discount.

Authorization Requested

The committee’s authorization is limited to two actions: (1) confirm Nimbus Data as the recommended award, and (2) authorize the category team to proceed into contract negotiation. No contract is executed by this approval; execution remains conditional on closing the SOC 2 condition and completing legal review.
