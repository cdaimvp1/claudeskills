What Happens Next To Close, Merged

Today the Recommendation subtab carries a condensed 4-stage "What happens next" stepper (Theo advisory → panel scoring → group decision → award) and the Business Case subtab separately carries a 6-step "What’s left to complete the deal" list (negotiation · legal & MSA · security & InfoSec · FRAP approval · purchase order · award). Marc asked for these merged into ONE richer section that lives on the Business Case tab: the advisory/decision stages AND the execution steps to close, in one flow, with owner, status, target window and blocker/dependency detail per step. The Recommendation subtab keeps only the small condensed stepper and points here for the full path. Three layout options below, same underlying data, same facts.
Grounded in the submitted seed data for this RFx (Analytics Platform Consolidation): the advisory recommendation is Nimbus Data, top panel score in the field, priced at $980,000/yr (31% off a $1,420,000 list, held 90 days) plus $210,000 fixed-fee implementation, capped at a 3%/yr escalator. It carries an open SOC 2 Type II Must-Have gate (a Type I report is on file; Type II is stated as in progress by the vendor, not yet evidenced). No prior Lilly MSA is on file for Nimbus (net-new); Nimbus has accepted Lilly’s paper as the base MSA and returned redlines, two open deviations, a liability cap below the Lilly floor and a data-residency carve-out. TPRM on Nimbus is under review with 2 open items. RFP intent records this as a Yellow CCI engagement, ~$3.2M TCO over 3 years, award subject to contract. Lakehouse Co ($1,060,000/yr) is on record as the clean conforming alternative: clears every Must-Have, has an existing MSA relationship on file, and is closest to signature. Target windows shown in every option below are illustrative relative sequencing, not scheduled dates, marked accordingly. NEEDS_INPUT: Helio Warehouse submitted no price, MSA or implementation plan, so it is not part of this close path and is out of scope for this panel.

Design-exploration mockup for the Business Case tab merge of "What happens next" (currently on the Recommendation subtab, a condensed 4-stage stepper) and "Path to close" / "What’s left to complete the deal" (currently on the Business Case subtab, a 6-step list). All three options above share one underlying 7-phase data set built from the seed for this RFx (Nimbus Data as the advisory recommendation, Lakehouse Co as the clean conforming alternative); only the layout differs. Colors follow the MCM palette: plum as primary (advisory layer, done), teal as secondary (execution layer), burnt orange as the single emphasis accent (current/active), muted blue for in-progress/partial status, deep rust reserved for the hard SOC 2 gate that blocks Award, grey for not-started. Not connected to live data and not part of the shipped dashboard.
