The award-scenario modeler from the Recommendation tab, re-laid out per review feedback. Category-weight sliders on the left drive a row of small ranking cards live; the fuller ranking read and the three scenario descriptions sit underneath as plain visible text, and the compact sensitivity radar closes out the panel with its own faint live polygon. Reflect-only: the panel's real weights and scores never change, this is a what-if sandbox layered on top of them.
Reading the data: Nimbus Data is the top panel score (4.4 of 5) at $980,000/yr but holds a SOC 2 Type I report against a Must-Have Type II gate, so it is excluded from the ranking regardless of weights. Lakehouse Co (4.2 of 5, $1,060,000/yr) clears every Must-Have. Helio Warehouse (3.2 of 5) has the strongest SAP integration but submitted no price, no MSA, and no implementation plan.

Design-exploration mockup, round 6, layout refinement on one panel ("Model the Decision" on the RFx Recommendation tab), built on the compact radar and scoring logic from RFx-RECOMMENDATION-OPTIONS-5.html and the weight profiles, fit matrix, and scenario descriptions from RFx-RECOMMENDATION-OPTIONS-4.html. Same data, weights, and scoring logic; the ranking is now a row of small cards across the top of the right column, with the fuller leader-and-lever read as visible text underneath, the three scenario descriptions as visible small text below that, and the same tight, bounding-box-fit radar last, now with a faint live polygon tracking the sliders on top of the three fixed reference scenarios. Not connected to live data and not part of the shipped dashboard. Colors follow the MCM palette: plum as primary, teal as secondary, burnt orange as the single emphasis accent, deep rust reserved for blocked or critical states, muted blue for neutral information.
