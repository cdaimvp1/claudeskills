Question (reframed)

For an $80,000/year professional-services engagement with no vendor access to Lilly data or systems, is a full MSA required, or is standard PO Terms & Conditions sufficient?

Answer

Standard PO Terms & Conditions are sufficient. A full MSA is not required for this engagement as described.

Details

  • Total contract value ($80,000/year) falls under the Playbook's PO-T&Cs ceiling for standalone services engagements (Section 4.2 sets that ceiling at $100,000 total value, including renewals).
  • No vendor access to Lilly data or systems was indicated for this use-case, so TPRM and Privacy Review are not triggered here.
  • This is a single-year engagement as described. If it becomes multi-year and cumulative value crosses $100,000, re-run this check: the threshold is evaluated on total value including renewals, not the annual figure alone.
  • FRAP does not apply: the Global Following FRAP Policy governs foreign-currency / cross-border payment-following arrangements, not standard domestic services procurement of this kind.
