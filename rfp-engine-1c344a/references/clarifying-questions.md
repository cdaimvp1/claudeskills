# Clarifying Questions - Domain-Specific Question Banks

Used in Step 3 of rfp-engine workflow. Ask only questions whose absence would materially degrade the RFP. Per Global Operating Rule 2, ask a maximum of 1 to 3 from any bank, batched once, never a long interview. Each list below is ordered roughly by materiality - work top-down and stop once you have enough to proceed. Universal questions (defined in SKILL.md) apply to all domains.

---

## Domain: Enterprise SaaS / Cloud Software

1. Is this a net-new capability or a replacement of an existing system? If replacement, who is the incumbent?
2. What is the expected user population - named users, concurrent users, or consumption-based?
3. Are there specific integration requirements with SAP S/4HANA, Microsoft 365, Veeva, or other Lilly systems?
4. What data residency requirements apply - US-only, EU, global?
5. What security certifications are mandatory - SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, FedRAMP, HITRUST?
6. Is there a pilot or POC planned before full deployment?
7. What is the anticipated contract term - 1 year, 3 years, 5 years?
8. Are there regulatory requirements affecting the deployment (GxP, HIPAA, 21 CFR Part 11)?

---

## Domain: Professional Services / Consulting

1. Is this a fixed-scope engagement or time-and-materials?
2. What are the geographic delivery expectations - Indianapolis-based, remote, hybrid?
3. Is there a preferred team size and seniority mix?
4. Are there any incumbent vendors currently doing similar work at Lilly?
5. What is the anticipated project duration?
6. Are there milestone-based payment triggers, or is this continuous billing?
7. Will the supplier have access to confidential Lilly data, systems, or IP?
8. Is there a specific methodology required (Agile, Lean, PMI, etc.)?

---

## Domain: Lab / Clinical Services

1. What sample types and volumes are anticipated?
2. Are GLP or GMP compliance standards required for this work?
3. What are the turnaround time requirements - standard and expedited?
4. Is this for pre-clinical, clinical Phase I-III, or commercial use?
5. What regulatory submissions will these services support (IND, NDA, BLA)?
6. Are chain-of-custody and sample tracking requirements specified?
7. Will the supplier need access to Lilly proprietary compounds or molecules?

---

## Domain: Hardware / Equipment

1. What is the deployment environment - lab, manufacturing floor, office, field?
2. What quantity is anticipated at initial deployment and at full scale?
3. Are there integration requirements with existing Lilly infrastructure?
4. What installation and commissioning support is required?
5. What maintenance model is preferred - preventive maintenance contract, break-fix, self-maintain?
6. What is the expected asset life - and are refresh cycles in scope?
7. Are there hazardous materials or special facility requirements?

---

## Domain: Chemicals / Raw Materials

1. What is the anticipated annual volume (kg, L, units) at initial and full scale?
2. Are custom synthesis or formulation capabilities required?
3. What purity, grade, or pharmacopeial specifications apply?
4. What regulatory documentation is required - COA, DMF, regulatory starting material?
5. What are the logistics requirements - cold chain, hazmat, controlled substance?
6. Is dual sourcing required for supply security?
7. Are there sustainability or green chemistry requirements?

---

## Domain: Technology Staffing / Staff Augmentation

1. What roles and seniority levels are needed?
2. What skill sets or certifications are required?
3. Is this on-site at Lilly, remote, or hybrid?
4. What is the anticipated duration and volume - FTEs, not hours?
5. Is a Master Service Agreement already in place with any candidates?
6. Are there specific tool or technology stack requirements?
7. Is there an incumbent supplier currently filling these roles?

---

## Always Ask If Not Evident (Universal - from SKILL.md)

- Target award timeline?
- Are legal terms (MSA/DPA) in scope for this RFP?
- Supplier invitation list - who will receive the RFP?
