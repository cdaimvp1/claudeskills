Opportunities and concerns

Opportunities

  • Separated storage and compute allows capacity to track demand rather than peak provisioning
  • Existing pharma reference base (Novartis, Sanofi, IQVIA) shortens regulated-environment proving
  • Native Iceberg support reduces lock-in exposure at the storage layer

Concerns

  • Consumption pricing makes spend a function of workload design, so cost control sits with Lilly, not the contract
  • Cyber posture for Lilly scope is not yet independently verified after the 2024 campaign
  • Runs on top of AWS, Azure or GCP, so hyperscaler concentration is inherited rather than removed
